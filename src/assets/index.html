Step-by-Step Process

Our service workflow is designed to ensure precision, compliance, and timely delivery. Below is a comprehensive outline of the process we follow:

I. Initial Documentation Collection
Our dedicated documentation team coordinates with the client to gather all necessary documents relevant to the service.
II. Assistance to prepare the Documents.
For documents that require a specific format, Corpseed provides the appropriate format to help prepare the documents correctly for the portal, reducing the chances of rejection.
III. Verification by Legal Experts
The collected documents are reviewed and verified by Corpseed's legal team to ensure accuracy, completeness, and compliance with regulatory requirements.
IV. Application & Document Drafting
Applications and supporting documents are drafted in accordance with the latest government-prescribed formats and guidelines.
V. Preparation for Submission
All documents and enclosures are organized and uploaded to the relevant government portal, ensuring adherence to submission protocols.
VI. Internal Scrutiny by Review Team
Our internal review team conducts a thorough pre-submission check to validate the application’s completeness and correctness.
VII. Client Review & Approval
The application is shared with the client for a final round of review and confirmation before official submission.
VIII. Online Submission to Government Authorities
Upon client approval, the application is formally submitted to the respective government department or authority.
